SnipSearch converted the detector and recognizer models to per-output-channel
INT8 weight storage. The models dequantize their weights for floating-point
inference. The upstream ONNX exports are from the RapidOCR model repository.
The dictionary is unchanged from PaddleOCR.

To regenerate the bundled models, run scripts/quantize-ocr-model.py with the
upstream ONNX file and the output path. The bundled files were generated with
ONNX 1.23.2 and NumPy 2.5.5.
