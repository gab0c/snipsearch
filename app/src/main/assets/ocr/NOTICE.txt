SnipSearch converted the detector and recognizer weights to per-output-channel
INT8 storage (dequantized for execution). The recognizer exports compact INT64
ArgMax token IDs and FLOAT maximum probabilities per time step; Softmax stays
internal to the graph, with no full character tensor copied to Kotlin. Upstream ONNX
exports and inference configurations come from the official PaddlePaddle
repositories on Hugging Face.

To regenerate the bundled models and dictionary from the upstream files:
  python3 scripts/quantize-ocr-model.py --extract-dict rec_inference.yml app/src/main/assets/ocr/ppocrv6_tiny_keys.txt
  python3 scripts/quantize-ocr-model.py det_inference.onnx app/src/main/assets/ocr/PP-OCRv6_tiny_det_infer.onnx --quantize-weights
  python3 scripts/quantize-ocr-model.py rec_inference.onnx app/src/main/assets/ocr/PP-OCRv6_tiny_rec_infer.onnx --quantize-weights --replace-softmax-with-argmax
